## Verification Metrics

- Survival: average survival time, failure distribution, hit rate, dodge success rate.
- Action: reaction window, input tolerance, hit area, recovery time.
- Economy: output, spend, inventory, inflation, paid/free differences.
- Level: clear rate, failure points, pacing breaks, retry count.

Unverified parameters must not be written as final values.
